CRPF TENDER NOTICE
Central Reserve Police Force
Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India

TENDER NO: CRPF/GC/CONST/2026/041
DATE: 01 April 2026
SUBJECT: Supply and Installation of Security Infrastructure Equipment

SECTION 1: INTRODUCTION
CRPF invites sealed bids from eligible contractors for supply, installation, testing and commissioning of security infrastructure equipment at CRPF Group Centre, Rampur, Uttar Pradesh.

SECTION 2: ESTIMATED VALUE
Total Estimated Cost: Rs. 4,50,00,000 (Rupees Four Crore Fifty Lakhs Only)
EMD: Rs. 9,00,000

SECTION 3: ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Section 3.1 - Financial Eligibility (Mandatory)
The bidder must have a minimum average annual turnover of Rs. 5,00,00,000 (Rupees Five Crore) during the last three consecutive financial years (2021-22, 2022-23, 2023-24) as certified by a Chartered Accountant.

Section 3.2 - Technical Experience (Mandatory)
The bidder must have successfully completed at least 2 (two) similar works in Central Government / State Government / PSU / Defence establishments during the last 7 years. Each completed work must be of value not less than Rs. 1,50,00,000.

Section 3.3 - GST Registration (Mandatory)
The bidder must hold a valid and active GST registration. GSTIN must be provided and will be verified. Cancelled or suspended GSTIN will lead to disqualification.

Section 3.4 - PAN Card (Mandatory)
Valid PAN Card in the name of the firm / company is mandatory. A self-attested copy must be submitted.

Section 3.5 - Quality Certification (Mandatory)
The bidder must hold a valid ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System certification from an accredited body. Certificate must be valid as on the date of submission.

Section 3.6 - MSME Registration (Optional)
Bidders registered under Udyam / MSME are encouraged to apply and may be given preference as per Government of India MSME procurement policy.

Section 3.7 - Solvency Certificate (Mandatory)
A solvency certificate from a scheduled commercial bank for a minimum amount of Rs. 1,00,00,000 issued not more than 6 months prior to submission date.

SECTION 4: DOCUMENT CHECKLIST
Bidders must submit the following:
1. Audited Balance Sheets for FY 2021-22, 2022-23, 2023-24
2. GST Registration Certificate with active GSTIN
3. PAN Card copy (self-attested)
4. ISO 9001:2015 Certificate (valid)
5. Work completion certificates for similar works
6. Solvency certificate from bank
7. Udyam Registration (if applicable)

SECTION 5: BID SUBMISSION
Last date for submission: 15 May 2026
Submit to: The Commandant, CRPF Group Centre, Rampur-244901, UP
